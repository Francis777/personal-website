I am a machine learning software engineer at [Wayve](https://wayve.ai). It's a unique experience to [reimagine an autonomous vehicle](https://wayve.ai/blog/a-new-approach-to-self-driving-av2-0/) here in London and I really enjoy collaborating with different teams to think about the solution from both data and model perspective.

I'm also interested in the theory of _learning to control from demonstration_ (e.g. imitation learning and inverse reinforcement learning) as well as its application on _pixel -> control_ tasks (e.g. autonomous driving!). For an abstract of this topic, I highly recommend [this blog post](https://www.argmin.net/2020/06/29/tour-revisited/) and [this talk](https://youtu.be/o7qENRDB2ug), both by Prof. Ben Recht.

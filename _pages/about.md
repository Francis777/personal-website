I am a machine learning software engineer at [Wayve](https://wayve.ai), it's a unique experience to [reimagine an autonomous vehicle](https://wayve.ai/blog/a-new-approach-to-self-driving-av2-0/) here in London.

I'm interested in the theory of _learning to control from demonstration_ and its application on pixel -> control tasks like autonomous driving. My favourite (brief) summaries on this topic are [this blog post](https://www.argmin.net/2020/06/29/tour-revisited/) and [this talk](https://youtu.be/o7qENRDB2ug), both by Prof. Ben Recht.

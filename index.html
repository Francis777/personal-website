I have long been interested in control from pixelsA collection of great talks that have shaped my research perspective.. While my primary focus is end-to-end autonomous driving, closing the loop between observation and action is a challenge shared across many domains. I draw inspiration from progress in other fields, from contact-rich robotic manipulation ($\pi^\star_{0.6}$) to computer-use agents (Yutori Navigator).

Before joining Wayve, I earned an M.S. in Mechanical Engineering at Stanford University, specializing in automatic control.
